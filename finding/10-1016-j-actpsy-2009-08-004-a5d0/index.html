Original paper · Acta Psychologica

The effects of conformity on recognition judgements for emotional stimuli

Brown & Schaefer, 2010 · doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2009.08.004

What people did

People looked at pictures that were positive, negative or neutral. Then they said which pictures they had seen before, mixed in with new ones. Another person, who was working with the researchers, gave wrong answers, correct answers or none.

What the original found

People's answers shifted toward what the other person said more for neutral pictures than for emotional ones.

In the original authors’ words: “Effects were larger for participants viewing neutral items and persisted for these items on a subsequent private source monitoring test.”
