The short answer

frozen-source comparison

Retested has 453 pairs in its built FORRT layer and 1,466 locally completed additions. Among the additions, 414 already have positive bibliographic same-pair evidence in the checked FORRT corpus. The remaining 1,052 do not yet have a confirmed same-pair relation—but that is an unfinished comparison, not proof that FORRT lacks them.

Across the whole site, 865 pairs have positive bibliographic same-pair evidence: 802 exact DOI pairs and 63 reviewed Work/version matches. Effect, study, sample and outcome equivalence have not yet been adjudicated, so the defensible count of certified new-to-FORRT effects or replications is still 0.

A “Work” is the underlying report; a journal article and its preprint can be different citable expressions of the same Work. Reviewed Work matches remain distinct records rather than being collapsed automatically.

What the FORRT inventory number means

not 11,168 unique high-quality effects

The frozen comparison corpus contains 11,168 original/later relationship records and 3,074 later-paper-only records across published, validated, extractor, pending, historical and unresolved FORRT layers. These are bibliographic source records. They are not a count of unique effects, independent samples, successful replications, or records that have all passed the same quality threshold.

Retested is an independent, effect-level explorer built on the open data of the FORRT Replication Hub (FReD and FLoRA). It is not an official FORRT product. The counted record also includes source-reviewed Retested additions. FORRT papers link to the Replication Atlas; every paper links to its primary source. Found a mistake or a missing replication?
