The record, as of the latest build

Psychology tested its famous findings again.

Of 7,399 replication attempts with a verdict, 47% found the original effect. 24% were mixed. 29% did not find it.

Retested follows each finding from the original claim to every attempt to repeat it: what the replicators said, what the numbers show, and how the attempts were run.

Three things the record shows

computed from coded study data
64%vs29%

A strong p-value predicts survival

Originals with p ≤ .005 replicated 64% of the time; those that only just cleared p < .05 did so 29% of the time. n = 154 and 117 original studies.

70%vs38%

Who replicates matters

When a replication team shared authors with the original, it succeeded 70% of the time. Fully independent teams: 38%. n = 92 and 649 attempts.

42%

Even survivors shrink

The typical replication found an effect 42% the size of the original's. 55% came in below half; 19% pointed the other way. 512 effects with sizes on both sides.

These are rates among studies people chose to replicate, which are often the surprising or doubted ones. They are not the replication rate of psychology as a whole. See the full record and its caveats.
