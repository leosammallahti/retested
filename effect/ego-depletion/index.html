Ego Depletion

7 original papers

The claim

Using willpower on one task drains a limited supply, so the next task needing self-control goes worse.

In the original authors' words: “Each of the recall measures offered strong support for the executive depletion hypothesis. Participants who had previously exercised attention control scored worse than participants who had not previously attempted attention control. Specifically,…”

Also filed as: An initial self-control task depletes later self-control · Ego depletion · Initial self-control impairs later self-control performance · Initial self-control reduces later task persistence · ego depletion

How the original study workedSmall cue that spills overmeasured as Speed, accuracy or memory

What people did

Some people were asked to eat radishes while chocolates were nearby, and others had no such demand. Then everyone tried puzzles that could not be solved.

What the original found

People who had to resist the chocolates and eat radishes quit the puzzles sooner.
