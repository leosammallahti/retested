Three environmental sensitivity groups in adolescents

Pluess et al., 2018

The claim

Teenagers fall into three distinct groups by how strongly their surroundings affect them: low, medium, or high sensitivity.

In the original authors' words: “the Latent Class Analysis suggests that there are 3 distinct groups with different levels of Environmental Sensitivity”

How the original study workedCompares existing traits or groupsmeasured as What people said or ratedChildren and teens

What people did

Children and teenagers in the UK filled out a short 12-question survey about how much they are shaped by the world around them. Researchers then used a statistical method to sort the answers into groups of similar kids.

What the original found

The answers fell into three distinct groups: low, medium and high sensitivity to surroundings.

In the original authors’ words: “the Latent Class Analysis suggests that there are 3 distinct groups with different levels of Environmental Sensitivity”

What happened when others tried it

  1. 2020
    Found itTillmann & MatanyEuropean Journal of Developmental Psychology

    What they did A total of 749 adolescents from all three typical types of schools in Germany (45% female, average age about 15) completed a shortened, translated 10-item version of the Highly Sensitive Person scale, a questionnaire measuring how sensitive people are to their surroundings. The team then looked for distinct groups of differently sensitive adolescents in the scores.

    What they found Results supported the existence of three sensitivity groups, which differed significantly from each other on their average scores. The authors present this as a replication of existing studies of sensitivity groups. The original used the 12-item Highly Sensitive Child scale in four UK-based samples, so this is a conceptual replication of the group count rather than a direct one, and one of the authors worked on both studies. The group sizes deviated from earlier findings: the low sensitive group was smaller, and the medium and high sensitivity groups were larger.
