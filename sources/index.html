What FORRT has, and what this site carries

Every row of every FORRT file this site reads, 20,809 in all, has one recorded outcome below. A build that leaves any row without one stops and publishes nothing.

In four numbers

  • 8,840 replication attempts are on the site: 6,183 from FORRT's files and 2,657 added by Retested, each read against its source paper.
  • 2,125 effects have at least one replication; 177 more are named in FORRT's records without one and are listed apart, not counted.
  • Read but not counted: 506 machine-found papers that two models read as replications of their linked original, but whose link a reader could not confirm from the text. Machine-found pairs are counted only once a reader has checked the link against both abstracts.
  • Unverified or without free text: 2,078 machine-found papers in the queue (from FORRT's extractor and from Retested's own search), and 597 rows of FORRT's Replications & Reversals list that cite a later paper without a resolved DOI or freely available text: many are replications, others are reviews or commentaries.

“On the site, linked to a different original” means the later paper is on the site, but FORRT's row names a different original paper for it than the one it is shown under. “Names an effect” rows carry no replication pair; they name an effect, and the effect's page holds whatever replications are linked to it.

Retested is an independent, effect-level explorer built on the open data of the FORRT Replication Hub (FReD and FLoRA). It is not an official FORRT product. The counted record also includes source-reviewed Retested additions. FORRT papers link to the Replication Atlas; every paper links to its primary source. Found a mistake or a missing replication? Submit it to FORRT, so everyone benefits.
