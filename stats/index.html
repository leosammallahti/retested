The record in numbers

7,399 replications, counted honestly

Every rate below is a share of replication attempts with a verdict (successful, mixed, or failed), with a 95% interval. Attempts are (original paper, replication paper) pairs from FLoRA, FReD, the legacy FReD file, and Retested-reviewed primary papers, so a multi-site project counts once per original it tested.

Read this first. Researchers choose what to replicate, and they pick surprising, influential or doubted results. These numbers describe that set of studies. They are not an estimate of how much of psychology would replicate if every study were tested.

There is also a record nobody counted: 1,211 candidate replications outside the counted record, 284 of them with an outcome. They are unverified and contribute nothing to the figures below, but they replicate at 44.4% against 47.2% here — worth reading beside this page.

How much effects shrink

512 effects with sizes on both sides

The median replication found 42% of the original effect. Half fell between 7% and 90%. 55% came in below half the original; 19% went the other way.

Retested is an independent, effect-level explorer built on the open data of the FORRT Replication Hub (FReD and FLoRA). It is not an official FORRT product.
