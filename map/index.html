How to read this

  • This is a pattern across many findings, not a verdict on any single one: a study of a small cue can hold up, and a therapy can fail.
  • Findings are not retested at random. Famous and surprising claims are retested more often, which shapes these figures.
  • Each effect was placed from a description of its original study with the retest results removed, by two AI models working separately, checked against a blind hand-coded sample; where the models disagreed, the placement was read and decided against the written rules. 2,124 of 2,125 effects are placed.

Retested is an independent, effect-level explorer built on the open data of the FORRT Replication Hub (FReD and FLoRA). It is not an official FORRT product. The counted record also includes source-reviewed Retested additions. FORRT papers link to the Replication Atlas; every paper links to its primary source. Found a mistake or a missing replication?
